PART D — WORD READING
========================================

Read aloud:

한국 - 한국 , 한국 .

한글 - 한글 , 한글 .


PART E — READING QUESTIONS
========================================

1. How many syllable blocks are in: 한국

Answer: 한 (first block/ syllable 1) , 국 (second block / syllable 2) .

2. How many syllable blocks are in: 한글

Answer: 한 (first block/ syllable 1) , 글 (second block / syllable 2) .

3. What does 한국 mean?

Answer: Korea

4. What does 한글 mean?

Answer: Korean Writing System
