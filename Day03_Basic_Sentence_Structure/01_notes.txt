========================================
KOREAN LANGUAGE JOURNEY
DAY 03 — BASIC SENTENCE STRUCTURE
========================================

GOAL:
Understand basic Korean sentence structure
and build simple Korean sentences.


----------------------------------------
1. KOREAN WORD ORDER
----------------------------------------

Korean commonly follows:

Subject + Object + Verb

S + O + V

English:

I eat rice.

Korean:

저는 밥을 먹어요.

Jeoneun babeul meogeoyo.

저는 = I
밥을 = rice
먹어요 = eat


----------------------------------------
2. VERB COMES AT THE END
----------------------------------------

This is one of the most important
rules in basic Korean.

English:

I study Korean.

Korean:

저는 한국어를 공부해요.

Jeoneun hangugeoreul gongbuhaeyo.

The verb comes at the end:

공부해요 = study


----------------------------------------
3. 은 / 는
----------------------------------------

은 / 는 marks the topic.

는 is used after a vowel.

나는
저는
친구는

은 is used after a consonant.

학생은
밥은
책은


----------------------------------------
4. 을 / 를
----------------------------------------

을 / 를 marks the object.

를 is used after a vowel.

커피를
한국어를

을 is used after a consonant.

밥을
책을
물을


----------------------------------------
5. BASIC SENTENCE PATTERN
----------------------------------------

Subject + Topic + Object + Verb

Example:

저는 밥을 먹어요.

Jeoneun babeul meogeoyo.

I eat rice.


Example:

저는 물을 마셔요.

Jeoneun mureul masyeoyo.

I drink water.


Example:

저는 한국어를 공부해요.

Jeoneun hangugeoreul gongbuhaeyo.

I study Korean.


----------------------------------------
6. IMPORTANT EXAMPLES
----------------------------------------

나는 밥을 먹어요.
I eat rice.

저는 물을 마셔요.
I drink water.

학생은 책을 읽어요.
The student reads a book.

친구는 한국어를 공부해요.
The friend studies Korean.


----------------------------------------
7. TOPIK POINT
----------------------------------------

When reading a Korean sentence,
look for the verb first.

The verb often appears at the end.

Example:

저는 한국어를 공부해요.

Verb = 공부해요

Then identify:

저는 = topic
한국어를 = object
공부해요 = verb
