PART 5 — MEMORY SPEAKING
----------------------------------------

Look at the English sentence.

Do NOT look at Korean.

Say the Korean sentence aloud.

1. I eat rice.

-> 저는 밥을 먹어요 .

2. I drink water.

-> 저는 믈을 마셔요 .

3. I study Korean.

-> 저는 한국어를 공부해요 .

4. The student reads a book.

-> 학생은 책을 읽어요 .

5. My friend listens to music.

-> 친구는 음악을 들어요 .
