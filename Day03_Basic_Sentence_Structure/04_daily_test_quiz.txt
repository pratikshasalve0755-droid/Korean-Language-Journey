PART 1 — DAILY TEST

Q1. What is the basic Korean sentence
word order?

Answer: subject + Object + verb


Q2. Where does the verb usually appear
in a basic Korean sentence?

Answer: at the end of the sentence .


Q3. What is the difference between
은 and 는?

Answer: 은 = it is topic marker , used after consonant  and 는 = it is used after vowel .


Q4. What is the difference between
을 and 를?

Answer: 을 = it marks the object , and is used after consonant  and 를 = it also marks the object ,
        and is used after the vowel.


Q5. Choose the correct particle:

나는 / 나은

Answer: 나는


Q6. Choose the correct particle:

학생은 / 학생는

Answer: 학생은


Q7. Choose the correct particle:

밥을 / 밥를

Answer: 밥을


Q8. Choose the correct particle:

한국어를 / 한국어을

Answer: 한국어를


Q9. Identify the topic, object and verb:

저는 밥을 먹어요.

Topic:    저는
Object:   밥을
Verb:     먹어요


Q10. Rearrange correctly:

먹어요 / 저는 / 밥을

Answer: 저는 밥을 먹어요

PART 2 — TOPIK-STYLE QUIZ
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━


Q1. Choose the correct sentence.

A. 저는 먹어요 밥을.
B. 저는 밥을 먹어요.
C. 밥을 저는 먹어요를.
D. 먹어요 저는 밥을.

Answer: B. 저는 밥을 먹어요.

Q2. Choose the correct particle.

저__ 학생이에요.

A. 는
B. 은
C. 를
D. 을

Answer: A. 는


Q3. Choose the correct particle.

밥__ 먹어요.

A. 는
B. 은
C. 을
D. 를

Answer: C. 을


Q4. What does this sentence mean?

저는 물을 마셔요.

A. I eat rice.
B. I drink water.
C. I study Korean.
D. I read a book.

Answer: B. I drink water.


Q5. Identify the verb.

친구는 음악을 들어요.

A. 친구는
B. 음악을
C. 들어요
D. 는

Answer: C. 들어요


Q6. Identify the object.

학생은 책을 읽어요.

A. 학생은
B. 책을
C. 읽어요
D. 은

Answer: 책을


Q7. Choose the correct Korean sentence
for:

"I study Korean."

A. 저는 한국어를 공부해요.
B. 저는 공부해요 한국어를.
C. 한국어는 저는 공부해요를.
D. 공부해요 한국어 저는.

Answer: A. 저는 한국어를 공부해요.


Q8. What is the main clue when identifying
the action in a basic Korean sentence?

A. The first word
B. The middle word
C. The final verb
D. The particle only

Answer: C. The final verb
