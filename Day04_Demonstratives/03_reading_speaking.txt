========================================
DAY 04 — READING + SPEAKING PRACTICE
========================================


----------------------------------------
PART 1 — READ ALOUD
----------------------------------------

Read each sentence 3 times.

1. 이것은 책이에요.
2. 그것은 물이에요.
3. 저것은 가방이에요.
4. 이거는 뭐예요?
5. 저거는 의자예요.
6. 이것은 연필이에요.
7. 그것은 핸드폰이에요.
8. 저것은 시계예요.


----------------------------------------
PART 2 — READING COMPREHENSION
----------------------------------------

Read each sentence and write its meaning.

1. 이것은 책이에요.

Meaning: This is a Book .

2. 그것은 물이에요.

Meaning: That is water .

3. 저것은 가방이에요.

Meaning: That over their is a Bag .

4. 이거는 뭐예요?

Meaning: what is this?

5. 이것은 연필이에요.

Meaning: that over their is pencil .


----------------------------------------
PART 3 — IDENTIFY THE WORD
----------------------------------------

For each sentence, identify the
demonstrative and the object.

1. 이것은 책이에요.

Demonstrative: 이것은
Object: 책

2. 그것은 물이에요.

Demonstrative: 그것은
Object: 물

3. 저것은 가방이에요.

Demonstrative: 저것은
Object: 가방

4. 이것은 연필이에요.

Demonstrative: 이것은
Object: 연필


----------------------------------------
PART 4 — SPEAKING PRACTICE
----------------------------------------

Say each sentence aloud 3 times.

이것은 책이에요.
그것은 물이에요.
저것은 가방이에요.
이거는 뭐예요?
저거는 의자예요.

Focus on clear syllables and natural rhythm.


----------------------------------------
PART 5 — MEMORY SPEAKING
----------------------------------------

Look at the English meaning.
Say the Korean sentence aloud.

1. This is a book.             -> 이것은 첵이에요 .
2. That is water.              -> 그갓은 물이에요 .
3. That over there is a bag.   -> 저것은 가방예요 .
4. What is this?               -> 이것은 뭬예요 .
5. This is a pencil.           -> 이것은 연필이에요 .


----------------------------------------
PART 6 — REAL-LIFE PRACTICE
----------------------------------------

Look around your room.

Choose 5 objects.

For each object, say a Korean sentence
using one of these:

이것은 ...
그것은 ...
저것은 ...

Example:

이것은 책이에요.

Write your 5 sentences:
