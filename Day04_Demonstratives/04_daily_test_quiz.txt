Q1. What does 이것 mean?

Answer: this thing


Q2. What does 그것 mean?

Answer: that thing


Q3. What does 저것 mean?

Answer: that thing over their   #Correction : That thing over there


Q4. What is the difference between 이 and 저?

Answer: 이 = it is used when the thing is near to the speaker and 저 = it is used when the thing is far from both
        speaker and listner


Q5. Translate: 책

Answer: Book


Q6. Translate: 가방

Answer: Bag


Q7. Complete the sentence:

______ 책이에요.
(This is a book.)

Answer: 이것은 책이에요.


Q8. Translate into Korean:

"What is this?"

Answer: 이거는 뭐예요?


Q9. Translate into English:

저것은 가방이에요.

Answer: That over there is a bag .


Q10. Write one Korean sentence using 이것.

Answer: 이것 맛있어요.

Q1. What does 이것 mean?

A. That thing over there
B. This thing
C. Water
D. Book

Answer: B. This thing


Q2. Choose the correct word for
"That thing over there."

A. 이것
B. 그것
C. 저것
D. 이거

Answer: C. 저것


Q3. What does 이거는 뭐예요? mean?

A. This is a book.
B. What is this?
C. That is water.
D. This is a pencil.

Answer: B. What is this?


Q4. Complete the sentence:

이것은 ______.

A. 책이에요
B. 먹어요
C. 마셔요
D. 공부해요

Answer: A. 책이에요


Q5. What does 책 mean?

A. Bag
B. Chair
C. Book
D. Water

Answer: C. Book


Q6. Which word means "pencil"?

A. 연필
B. 의자
C. 가방
D. 학교

Answer: A. 연필


Q7. Choose the correct translation:

"That over there is a bag."

A. 이것은 책이에요.
B. 저것은 가방이에요.
C. 그것은 물이에요.
D. 이거는 뭐예요?

Answer: B. 저것은 가방이에요.


Q8. Which expression asks
"What is this?"

A. 이것은 책이에요.
B. 저것은 가방이에요.
C. 이거는 뭐예요?
D. 그것은 물이에요.

Answer: C. 이거는 뭐예요?
